PilotlightExample page, invented product

A status page your customers can read without asking you.

Pilotlight checks your public URLs every minute and writes a plain page that says what is up, what is down and since when. It does not do anything else.

Read how a check works

What one check records

Every minute, from three places, it requests the URL and keeps the result. A check fails if the response takes longer than ten seconds, returns a 5xx status, or presents a certificate that has expired.

What it will not do

It will not guess at causes, page you in the night or draw a chart of your response times. If you want those, use something larger. The checks are in one file, checks.toml, and the page is plain HTML you can host anywhere.

Pilotlight is a fictional product used to show a page built with the anti-slop-design skill.
