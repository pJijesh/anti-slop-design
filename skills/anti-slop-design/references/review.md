# Review

The linter catches patterns. These checks catch what it cannot.

1. **Squint test.** Blur your eyes or the screenshot. Can you tell what the page is for and where to act? If everything has equal weight, the hierarchy is missing.
2. **Cover-the-logo test.** Hide the name. Could this be any of a hundred other companies? If yes, the page has no point of view. Add the specific thing.
3. **The sentence test.** Read the headline and first paragraph aloud. Would a person in this trade say it? If it reads like an advert for advertising, rewrite it.
4. **The delete test.** Remove each element in turn. If nothing is lost, remove it for good.
5. **The 390 test.** Render at 390 pixels wide. No sideways scroll, no overlapping text, first screen still makes sense, tap targets at least 44 pixels.
6. **The keyboard test.** Tab through the page. Focus is always visible and the order makes sense.
7. **The grey test.** Convert to greyscale. Is hierarchy and meaning still clear? State must not depend on colour alone.
8. **The facts test.** Point to the source of every figure and name. Remove any you cannot.

## Reporting

State the direction chosen and why, the checks run, the tells deliberately kept with their reasons, and what could not be checked (for example, no browser was available to render the page).
