## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-11

### Added

- The anti-slop-design skill: a process, a floor, and ten short references.
- A zero-dependency linter with 29 rules in three tiers, overrides with a required reason, JSON output and exit codes.
- Installers for Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and Cursor, with generated adapters.
- A before and after example pair, rendered and scored by the linter.
- Community files, citation metadata and a Dependabot configuration.
